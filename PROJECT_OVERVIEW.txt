┌─ KEY METRICS & CALCULATIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                              │
│  Savings Rate:          (Monthly Income - Expenses) / Monthly Income × 100  │
│  Expense Ratio:         (Total Expenses / (Income × 3)) × 100              │
│  Health Score:          80+ (Excellent) | 60-79 (Good) | 40-59 (Fair)     │
│  Retirement Savings:    FV = PV(1.07)^n + PMT × [((1.07)^n - 1) / 0.07]   │
│  Anomaly Detection:     Flag if Amount > Mean + (2 × StdDev)               │
